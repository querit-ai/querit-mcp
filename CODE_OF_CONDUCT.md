# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific in technical discussions.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Accept feedback gracefully and keep criticism focused on the work.
- Protect credentials, private user data, and vulnerability details.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and applied consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method published by the Querit GitHub organization. Do not include personal information in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
